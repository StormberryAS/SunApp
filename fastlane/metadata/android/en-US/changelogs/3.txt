1.1.1 corrects the footer and makes it useful.

Fixed
- The footer claimed "No permissions", which was not true. The APK declares nine, all of
  them for the alarm feature. It now reads "Alarm permissions only. No internet. No
  tracking.", which is accurate: there is genuinely no INTERNET permission, so the app
  cannot reach the network. This was corrected in the source on 4 September but the fix
  had never been released, so every installed copy still showed the old wording.

Added
- Privacy and Disclaimer are now links in the footer, so the terms are reachable from
  inside the app rather than only from the website.
- The Stormberry logo in the footer opens stormberry.as.

Opening any of those hands the address to your browser. The app still has no INTERNET
permission and still cannot reach the network itself.
