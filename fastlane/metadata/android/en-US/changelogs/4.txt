1.1.2 changes the typeface.

- Uses the Inter typeface throughout the app, the same one the website uses. The fonts
  are bundled inside the app, so nothing is downloaded and the app still has no
  INTERNET permission. Clock times keep their fixed-width digits so they line up.
- On narrow phones, the example in the empty city search field no longer wraps onto a
  second line, so the field keeps its height when you start typing.
